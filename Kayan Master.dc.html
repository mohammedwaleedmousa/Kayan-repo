01 — THE INSTITUTION

The missing middle

In Arabic, كِيان means an entity — a thing with structure and substance. Two chains were broken: talent in Yemen could not reach serious clients, and serious clients could not verify unknown talent. Kayan is the institutional layer between them, and its mission is that geography stops being destiny.

THE ONE SENTENCE · used verbatim, everywhere

Kayan is the managed delivery company that connects verified Yemeni talent with global clients — with escrow protection, milestone accountability, and a financial guarantee.

THE IRON RULE
No signed scope + no funded guarantee = no work.

Printed on the model page, repeated at intake, enforced at gate G2. It is the one sentence a client may quote back at us.

WE SELL OUTCOMES
Kayan does not sell effort.

No hourly rates on any public surface. Every commercial page quotes a scope and a date. Effort is our problem; the outcome is the product.

DIGNITY FIRST
Describe people by what they now do.

Never by what they once lacked. Impact is reported by name and number, never by pity. This governs every photograph in the system.

MISSION AND MARGIN
The same activity, not two budgets.

Kayan is not a charity with a business attached. The civic line runs on the same rails as the money, funded from operating surplus.

The three fractures, and what repairs each

Yemen's share of the six-trillion-dollar global professional services market is under a hundredth of one per cent. That is not a capability gap; it is a trust deficit with three named fractures.

THE FRACTURETHE OPERATIONAL FAILURETHE STRUCTURAL CORRECTION
Client & providerClients cannot verify providers or enforce delivery.Managed delivery: one accountable contract with a bank-held guarantee.
Funder & outcomeDonors fund training but cannot trace graduates into paid work.The absorption layer: a chain from Forge training to recorded, bankable income.
Talent & marketCapable professionals cannot prove credentials or identity globally.The Verified Talent Map, expressed through the Kayan Card.

Neither a marketplace nor an agency

NOT A MARKETPLACE

A marketplace introduces strangers and withdraws, leaving the client to be recruiter, project manager and risk absorber — paying once in money and again in uncertainty. It sells access to a crowd.

Kayan sells a guaranteed outcome, and takes responsibility for who the people are.

NOT AN AGENCY

An agency hides its margin, carries a fixed bench that is expensive to maintain and slow to adapt, and rests its trust on a relationship with one person.

Kayan publishes its split, assembles a pod per engagement, and makes the institution — not an individual — liable for the fix.

THE STANDING PROMISE · الوعد الراسخ
Geography is no longer destiny.

A developer in Aden or a researcher in Taiz should deliver with the same dignity and access as any professional on earth. And operating in a demanding environment is treated as a specialisation, not a handicap: delivering quality through power cuts and connectivity gaps is itself a discipline, backed by solar and satellite infrastructure. Kayan succeeds when a global client's first reflex is to reach for it — not to help Yemen, but because they want the work done well.

03 — THE BIRD DOCTRINE

One bird. Six flights.

Kayan does not keep a zoo. There is one bird — the Socotra Golden-winged Grosbeak, the messenger of capability — and each business line is signalled by how it flies, not by swapping species. The flight path is the line's motion signature: it governs the masthead animation, the loading state, and the way a card enters the screen.

THE MESSENGER OF CAPABILITY

Socotra Golden-winged Grosbeak

Rhynchostruthus socotranus

Endemic to Socotra: a heavy-billed finch, grey-brown with a black mask and a broad golden-yellow flash across the wing and tail. That gold panel is the reason it is the mark — capability visible only in motion, when the wing opens.

It carries skill from where it is to where it is needed. Its flight paths across the site are the trails of projects connecting Aden to the world.

ONE BIRD RULE

No second species is ever introduced. A line is signalled by its flight path and its bloom colour, never by a different animal.

PHOTOGRAPH OR MARK

Where a real bird is shown it is a licensed photograph of the species. Everywhere else, the mark. The grosbeak is never hand-illustrated or re-drawn.

MOTION BUDGET

One flight per screen. The path draws once on entry, then rests — it does not loop behind text a user is trying to read.

15 — THE STACK · TO BUILD

Boring technology, chosen for a bad connection

Two constraints govern every choice: it must work on a mid-range Android over an unreliable Yemeni connection, and a small team must maintain it without a specialist. Nothing here is chosen because it is new.

FRONT END
Server-rendered, hydrated only where needed

Marketing pages ship as static HTML with no framework runtime. Interactivity is added per component, not per page. Logical CSS properties throughout, so the RTL mirror is structural rather than a stylesheet fork.

TYPE DELIVERY
Self-hosted, subset per script

Amiri, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Outfit and Plex Mono as self-hosted WOFF2, subset and split so no page downloads a script it does not render.

PORTAL
One relational database, strict states

Gate state is a single enumerated column with an append-only audit table beside it: every transition records who, when, and against which artefact. No state is edited in place.

MONEY
Bank-held liability, never our balance

Funds sit under GL 2100 at Aden Bank. The portal records the escrow state and reference only; release is a human action by Finance against a written acceptance.

DOCUMENTS
Generated, versioned, never hand-edited

Scopes, change orders and handover minutes are produced from templates with a reference number and stored immutably. A signed document is superseded by a new version that cites it — never replaced.

AI, WHERE IT EARNS ITS PLACE
Drafting and matching, never deciding

The intake engine drafts a scope from an unstructured brief; predictive matching proposes pod combinations from the register. Both produce suggestions a named human signs. No model closes a gate.

IDENTITY & ACCESS
Separation enforced in the schema

Clients see only their own project. Reviewers cannot see commercial terms. Resourcing cannot alter verification records. Nobody verifies their own hire.

MEASUREMENT
Server-side, cookieless

Page views, intake submissions, gate durations. No third-party trackers and no session recording on a surface that handles identity documents.

THE RULE BEHIND ALL OF IT
If it cannot be audited, it is not built

Every feature answers two questions: what record does this leave, and who can read it? A feature that leaves no record has no place in an institution whose product is proof.
