# POD FORMATION & DOCUMENTATION SOP · KAY-MD-SOP-001
Classification: Internal · Owner: POS-DEL · Conforms to KAY-REG-FACTS-001

## 1 · Two ways a delivery team forms
A) ASSEMBLED — POS-DEL composes from the verified map at G4 (default).
B) SELF-LINKED (Ready Pod) — 2–6 verified talents link themselves under one
   Pod ID (requested at registration or later via profile). Rules:
   · every member individually T2+ in a needed micro-spec
   · one member nominated lead (T3 preferred)
   · POS-DEL approves composition; Assurance Score computed as a unit (≥70)
   · a Ready Pod appears in the G4 map as ONE selectable team — the fastest
     route from talent to revenue, and the members' incentive to recruit
     each other.

## 2 · Pod bonus
When a commission closes at G9 clean (no warranty claims, documentation pack
complete, evaluation composite ≥4.0 for the pod), a pod bonus of 2% of the
talent allocation is released to the pod, split by the lead's recorded shares.
Funded from Kayan's fee — quality literally pays.

## 3 · The documentation duty (the 5% reserve)
5% of the team's final release is HELD at G8 and released only when the
Documentation Pack is complete within 7 days of G7. This is stated in the
engagement terms — it is a duty, not a surprise.

THE PACK (checklist = workbook tab 06):
· kickoff minutes + checkpoint logs (already exist if gates were run)
· HAND-OFF MOMENTS: a photo or short clip each time work passes between
  members (screenshot of the send, or a 10-second capture)
· WORK-SESSION CAPTURES: at least one photo/screen-recording per week of the
  pod working — online call screenshot or Hub table photo
· FINAL WALKTHROUGH CLIP: 60–120s, the lead walks through the deliverable
· CONSENT FLAGS: each item marked client-visible YES/NO (only YES items may
  feed cases & social)

WHY: the packs are the proof engine (cases, social, INGO evidence), the
model's memory, and the talent's own portfolio. Capture is designed light:
phone photos and screenshots qualify; polish is VIS-01's job, not the pod's.

## 4 · Working together — sequencing & simultaneity
At kickoff the lead declares the mode per workstream:
· SEQUENTIAL (A finishes → B starts): hand-off moment REQUIRED at each pass.
· SIMULTANEOUS (parallel workstreams): weekly sync REQUIRED, minuted (3 lines:
  done/next/blockers), plus one work-session capture.
Disputes inside a pod go to the lead first, then POS-DEL — never to the client.

## 5 · Evaluation (feeds the Talent line)
At G9, POS-DEL scores each member 1–5 on: delivery vs plan · quality ·
communication · documentation duty · client feedback echo (workbook tab 07).
Composite ≥4.5 → commendation on the card record. ≤2.5 on any axis →
tier review with written reasons. Every score is visible to the member.
