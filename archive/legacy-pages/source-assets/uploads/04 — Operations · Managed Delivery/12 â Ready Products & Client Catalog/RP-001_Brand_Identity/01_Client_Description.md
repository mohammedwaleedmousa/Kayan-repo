# Brand Identity Pack — what you get
*(RP-001 · client-facing description · draft for kayanwork.com)*

A complete, usable brand — in fourteen days.

Not just a logo. A whole visual identity your business can actually run on: a logo
suite in every format you'll need, your colours and typefaces chosen and documented,
a brand guide that shows you and anyone you hire exactly how to use it, your
stationery, and a starter kit for social media — all designed by a Kayan-verified
designer, checked independently before it reaches you, with your payment held safely
until you accept the work.

## What arrives
- A logo suite — primary, secondary, and mark — in AI, SVG, and PNG, ready for print and screen
- Your colour palette and typography, chosen for your business and documented
- A 12-page brand guide (PDF) — usage, spacing, what to do and what never to do
- Stationery — business card and letterhead, print-ready
- A social starter kit — profile image, cover, and three post templates you can edit

## How it works
1. A 45-minute discovery call to understand your business and your taste.
2. On day 4, two design directions — you choose the one that's right.
3. On day 9, the chosen direction developed into the full system.
4. Independent quality check, then delivery on day 13.
5. You have five days to review; we're not done until you're happy.

## The guarantee
Your payment is protected in a supervised bank account and released only when you
accept the work. One free revision round on the direction you choose is included.

## Price
$500, fixed. (Rush delivery +10%. Extra revision rounds +20%.)

## Common questions
- *Can you also do naming or trademark?* Not in this pack — we'll point you the right way.
- *Do I own the files?* Yes, fully, with the source files handed over.
- *What if I don't like either direction?* We talk; the direction is always your choice,
  and we document it together on day 4 so we build the right thing.
