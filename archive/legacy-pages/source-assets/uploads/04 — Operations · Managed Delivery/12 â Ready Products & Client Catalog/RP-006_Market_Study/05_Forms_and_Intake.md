# Intake & forms — Market Study
*(RP-006 · the intake form + brief + SOW annex for this product)*

## The client intake form
Captured on the website when a client requests this product, or filled with them on a
scoping call. Every field feeds the SOW and the pod brief automatically.

**1. The decision**
   *What decision will this study inform? (This is the most important question.)*   `[text]`

**2. The market**
   *What product, service, or sector, and in which geography?*   `[text]`

**3. The questions**
   *What do you most need to know? List your top questions.*   `[text]`

**4. What you have**
   *Any existing data, reports, or contacts we should start from?*   `[file]`

**5. Interviews**
   *Are there specific people or organisations you want us to interview?*   `[text]`

**6. The deadline**
   *When do you need the decision brief in hand?*   `[date]`

## The brief (built from the intake)
The Researcher turns the intake into a one-page pod brief: the goal, the scope in and out,
the milestones, the acceptance lines, and any flags. Reviewed with the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
This product attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work that states:
the fixed price ($2200), the 14-day timeline, the deliverables listed in
the client description, the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one included revision round,
and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work starts; funds confirmed at G3.
