## Common questions
**How do you find respondents in Yemen?**
We seed the interview panel from the Kayan network and the Hub community, and triangulate where access is hard — and we say so plainly rather than dress a thin sample as a full one.

**What if the data just isn't there?**
We state it. Where numbers are scarce, we triangulate and show our method — no figure appears without its provenance. That's the house rule.

**Can this feed a business plan or pitch?**
Yes — it leads naturally into our Financial Model and Pitch Deck products.

**How current is it?**
The desk research and interviews are done fresh for your scope, in the fourteen-day window.
