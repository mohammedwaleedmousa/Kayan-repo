Family: Research, Data & Evaluation · Pod: Researcher T3+ · Analyst · QA · Delivery: 14 days

6 ACCEPTANCE: every figure sourced or labelled estimate with method · all scoped questions answered or honestly bounded · decision brief actionable stand-alone
