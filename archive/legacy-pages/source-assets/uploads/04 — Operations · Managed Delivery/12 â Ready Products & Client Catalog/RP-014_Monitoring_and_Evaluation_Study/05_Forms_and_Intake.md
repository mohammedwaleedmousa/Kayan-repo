# Intake & forms — Monitoring & Evaluation Study
*(RP-014 · the intake form + brief + SOW annex for this product)*

## The client intake form
Captured on the website when a client requests this product, or filled with them on a
scoping call. Every field feeds the SOW and the pod brief automatically.

**1. The programme**
   *What is being evaluated? Share the programme document and logframe.*   `[file]`

**2. The purpose**
   *What is this evaluation for — accountability, learning, a donor requirement?*   `[select]`

**3. The questions**
   *What are the key evaluation questions you need answered?*   `[text]`

**4. The criteria**
   *Any specific OECD-DAC criteria or donor requirements to prioritise?*   `[text]`

**5. Data & access**
   *What data exists, and who can we speak with? Any field-access constraints?*   `[text]`

**6. Safeguarding**
   *Are there sensitive or safeguarding topics we should plan for?*   `[text]`

**7. The deadline**
   *When is the evaluation due to the donor?*   `[date]`

## The brief (built from the intake)
The M&E lead turns the intake into a one-page pod brief: the goal, the scope in and out,
the milestones, the acceptance lines, and any flags. Reviewed with the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
This product attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work that states:
the fixed price ($4800), the 30-day timeline, the deliverables listed in
the client description, the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one included revision round,
and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work starts; funds confirmed at G3.
