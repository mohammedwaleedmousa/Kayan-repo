# Pod playbook — Monitoring & Evaluation Study
*(RP-014 · internal — how the pod delivers, step by step)*

## The pod
The pod (lead = M&E lead):
  - M&E lead (T4): 40 hours
  - Analyst (—): 28 hours
  - QA (—): 4 hours
The M&E lead leads: owns the client relationship, the scope, and the final sign-off.

## The milestones
- **D1** — Matrix locked · client: Client confirms matrix · evidence: Signed evaluation matrix
- **D8** — Desk review memo · client: — · evidence: Interim memo
- **D18** — Fieldwork complete · client: — · evidence: Field log
- **D24** — Draft report · client: — · evidence: Draft report
- **D26** — Validation session · client: Client validation · evidence: Validation log
- **D28** — QA layer-3 · client: — · evidence: QA sign-off
- **D29** — Submission · client: Client receives report · evidence: Submission letter
- **+5 days** — Acceptance window · client: 5-day silence = acceptance · evidence: Acceptance / revision request

## Definition of done
The work is done when every acceptance line below is met and the independent QA layer has
signed off at 4.0 or above. Nothing goes to the client in the submission letter until then.

## The honesty clause
This product carries Kayan's honesty clause: any gap in evidence, data, or scope is flagged
the day it's found and never papered over. Honest delivery protects the client and Kayan —
it is the same standard Kayan applies to its own impact reporting.

## Escalation
If quality or deadline is at risk, the M&E lead escalates to the MD Lead immediately.
Funds for any undelivered milestone are never released.
