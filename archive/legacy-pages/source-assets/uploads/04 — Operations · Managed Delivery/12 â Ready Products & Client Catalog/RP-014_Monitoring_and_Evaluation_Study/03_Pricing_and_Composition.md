# Pricing & pod composition — Monitoring & Evaluation Study
*(RP-014 · internal — reconciles to the Locked Facts, Pod Economics, and Finance OS)*

## Price
Client price: $4800 USD, fixed.
Rush delivery (compressed to 24 days): +10%.
Additional revision rounds beyond the one included: +20% each.

How the money is handled (reconciles to the Locked Facts and the Payments doctrine):
- Funds are 100% confirmed and protected before work starts (gate G3) — held in the
  supervised bank-held account, released on milestone acceptance. Never cash, never informal.
- Revenue is recognised only at client acceptance (gate G7); five days of silence after
  the submission letter counts as acceptance.
- A 2% quality holdback is released 30 days after acceptance (gates G8/G9).
- The pod is paid via the 80/20/2 split at the gates (80% to the doers, 20% to the lead
  layer, 2% to the quality holdback pool), reconciled to the Finance OS.

Kayan's revenue is the agent fee embedded in the price (the 15–22% band), not the gross
project value — Kayan recognises only its commission as revenue.

## The pod (lead = M&E lead):
  - M&E lead (T4): 40 hours
  - Analyst (—): 28 hours
  - QA (—): 4 hours

## Delivery window
30 standard days plus a 5-day client acceptance window.
Rush option compresses delivery to 24 days at +10%.

## What each dollar buys
The price covers the verified pod's time, the independent quality layer, and Kayan's
managed-delivery wrapper — scoping, milestone protection, QA, and the acceptance/warranty
process. The client pays once, fixed; the pod is paid at the gates via the 80/20/2 split.

## Margin discipline
Priced to the frontier-market rate for this service (validated in the Wide Research
market-demand workstream), with Kayan's agent fee embedded. The gross margin on Kayan's
fee is tracked in the MD Control Workbook against the ≥ target; this product is not run
below the margin floor.
