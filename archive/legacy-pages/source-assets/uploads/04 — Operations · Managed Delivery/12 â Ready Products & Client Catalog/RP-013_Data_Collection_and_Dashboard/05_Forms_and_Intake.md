# Intake & forms — Data Collection & Dashboard
*(RP-013 · the intake form + brief + SOW annex for this product)*

## The client intake form
Captured on the website when a client requests this product, or filled with them on a
scoping call. Every field feeds the SOW and the pod brief automatically.

**1. The questions**
   *What do you need to measure, and why? What decisions will the data inform?*   `[text]`

**2. The indicators**
   *Do you have indicators already, or should we design them with you?*   `[select]`

**3. The population & sample**
   *Who are you collecting from, where, and roughly how many?*   `[text]`

**4. Field context**
   *What should we know about access, security, or timing in the collection area?*   `[text]`

**5. Consent & data**
   *Any specific consent or data-protection requirements from your donor or organisation?*   `[text]`

**6. The deadline**
   *When do you need the dashboard live?*   `[date]`

## The brief (built from the intake)
The Data analyst turns the intake into a one-page pod brief: the goal, the scope in and out,
the milestones, the acceptance lines, and any flags. Reviewed with the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
This product attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work that states:
the fixed price ($3200), the 21-day timeline, the deliverables listed in
the client description, the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one included revision round,
and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work starts; funds confirmed at G3.
