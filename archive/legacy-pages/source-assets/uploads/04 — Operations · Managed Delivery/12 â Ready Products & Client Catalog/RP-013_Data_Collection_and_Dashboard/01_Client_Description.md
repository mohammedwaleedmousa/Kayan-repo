## Common questions
**How do you handle field access and security?**
A local field lead plans the routes and calls no-go areas honestly — we never force completion where it isn't safe. Honesty over a padded sample.

**How is consent handled?**
A consent script is embedded in the form itself, and refusals are logged. Personal data is handled per our data policy — aggregate reporting, protected storage.

**Can our team keep using the dashboard?**
Yes — the 90-minute handover training is built in, and the dashboard refreshes from the data on its own. It's yours to run.

**Is the data disaggregated?**
Sex, age, and governorate disaggregation is built into the instrument from the start, where relevant.
