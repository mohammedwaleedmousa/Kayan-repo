# Intake & forms — NGO Report & Proposal Pack
*(RP-012 · the intake form + brief + SOW annex for this product)*

## The client intake form
Captured on the website when a client requests this product, or filled with them on a
scoping call. Every field feeds the SOW and the pod brief automatically.

**1. The donor & the call**
   *Who is the donor, and do you have the call document, template, or logframe?*   `[file + text]`

**2. Report or proposal**
   *Is this a results report on work already done, or a proposal for new funding?*   `[select]`

**3. The deadline**
   *When must this be submitted? (This sets whether the rush rule applies.)*   `[date]`

**4. The evidence**
   *What evidence, data, or documents do you have for the results or the case? Upload what you can.*   `[file]`

**5. Length & language**
   *Roughly how long, and do you need Arabic, English, or both?*   `[select]`

**6. The logframe**
   *Do you have a logframe or results framework we should write to?*   `[file]`

**7. The reviewer**
   *Who on your side reviews and signs before submission?*   `[text]`

## The brief (built from the intake)
The Writer turns the intake into a one-page pod brief: the goal, the scope in and out,
the milestones, the acceptance lines, and any flags. Reviewed with the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
This product attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work that states:
the fixed price ($1400), the 12-day timeline, the deliverables listed in
the client description, the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one included revision round,
and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work starts; funds confirmed at G3.
