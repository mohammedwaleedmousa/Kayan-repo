Document: KAY-CON-012 | v2.0 | 2026-02-22
⚠ REQUIRES ADEN/YEMEN COUNSEL VALIDATION | تحتاج مراجعة محامٍ مرخص
1. MEMBERSHIP TYPES | 1. أنواع العضوية
1.1 Day Pass: Single-day access to open workspace, Wi-Fi, and shared amenities.
1.2 Hot Desk Membership: Monthly access to open workspace area (no reserved desk).
1.3 Dedicated Desk Membership: Monthly access to a reserved desk in shared space.
1.4 Private Office: Monthly access to a private office suite (subject to availability).
1.5 Meeting Room: Hourly/daily booking for meeting and event spaces.
2. MEMBERSHIP OBLIGATIONS | 2. التزامات العضوية
2.1 Members agree to: (a) comply with the Workspace Code of Conduct; (b) treat all equipment and facilities with care; (c) maintain a clean and professional environment; (d) respect other members' privacy and work; (e) comply with the Wi-Fi Acceptable Use Policy (KAY-CON-H05); (f) not use the space for unlawful purposes; (g) comply with Kayan's Safeguarding Policy; (h) not operate in or support Houthi-controlled territory activities from the workspace.
2.1 يوافق الأعضاء على: (أ) الالتزام بمدونة سلوك مساحة العمل؛ (ب) العناية بالمعدات والمرافق؛ (ج) الحفاظ على بيئة نظيفة ومهنية؛ (د) احترام خصوصية الأعضاء الآخرين؛ (هـ) الالتزام بسياسة الاستخدام المقبول للإنترنت؛ (و) عدم استخدام المكان لأغراض غير مشروعة.
3. PAYMENT | 3. الدفع
3.1 Monthly memberships: Payment due on the 1st of each month, or on the membership start date.
3.2 Day passes: Payment before entry.
3.3 Meeting rooms: Payment upon booking confirmation.
3.4 All prices are in USD. Payment in YER accepted at the prevailing rate.
3.5 Late payment: Membership may be suspended after 7 days of non-payment. 14 days = automatic termination.
4. FACILITIES AND AMENITIES | 4. المرافق والخدمات
4.1 Included: Wi-Fi, electricity (solar-backed), shared kitchen, printing (fair use), common areas, café access, community events.
4.2 Additional charges apply for: excessive printing, private events, catering, storage lockers, and mail handling.
5. LIABILITY | 5. المسؤولية
5.1 Kayan is not liable for loss or damage to members' personal property. Members are advised to use lockers and not leave valuables unattended.
5.2 Members are liable for any damage they cause to Kayan's property.
6. TERMINATION | 6. الإنهاء
6.1 Monthly memberships: Either party may terminate with 15 days' written notice before the next billing cycle.
6.2 Immediate termination for: code of conduct violations, non-payment >14 days, or any activity that threatens the safety of other members.
7. PHOTO/VIDEO | 7. التصوير
7.1 Kayan may photograph or video the workspace for promotional purposes. Members who do not wish to be photographed must submit a written opt-out using the Photo/Video Consent Form (KAY-CON-H06).
