1. Standing instructions
These govern how every Kayan asset is produced. They are settled and are not re-derived at the start of each session.
#
Instruction
SI-01
Define before derive. Nothing downstream is built until the layer above it is settled. The order is: positioning, business lines, entities, identity, taxonomy, forms, workflow, pages, automation, procedure.
SI-02
Every deliverable ships as a complete unit: the definition, its forms, its controlled lists, its procedure and checklist, and its automation specification. A definition without its procedure is not finished.
SI-03
Automation-first, manual by exception. A human touches a record only at a genuine judgement gate. Any process needing routine manual handling is designed wrong.
SI-04
One real-world thing, one record, one identifier, one page. Everything else is a view.
SI-05
Every material claim is labelled FACT, JUDGEMENT, ASSUMPTION, RECOMMENDATION or OPEN. Nothing is asserted as settled that is not.
SI-06
No placeholders, no outlines, no generic templates. Every document is final-draft and operable by someone who was not present when it was written.
SI-07
Numbers live in controlled documents, never in foundational ones. Changing a price must never require reopening a constitution.
SI-08
Red-team every material recommendation before presenting it. State the residual risk where the counter is insufficient.
SI-09
Historical documents are evidence, never authority. Mine them for logic; never adopt them because they exist.
SI-10
Decisions are recorded in K-00-02 at the moment they are taken. An undocumented decision is not a decision.
SI-11
Work in bounded sessions. Each session states what it will build, builds it completely, verifies it, and records what is next.
SI-12
Verify before shipping. Render every document and look at it. Recalculate every workbook and check the values are right, not merely error-free.
SI-13
Formatting is deliberately plain until the identity system is agreed. Structure carries the meaning; decoration is added later, once.
SI-14
Lead. Where the founder is thinking aloud, extract the signal, choose the strongest defensible path, execute it, and record it as a decision for ratification.

3.2 Material judgements taken in session 1
The verification and trust layer is the core asset; managed delivery is how it is monetised. This reframes Kayan from a service company to an infrastructure company, and it is the single most consequential position taken.
The taxonomy is widened from twelve digital-centric families to twenty covering all work. The earlier structure was defining who Kayan was for by omission.
Verification is formally severed from work allocation, and this is stated to registrants twice. It is what makes honest registration rational.
The payment and fund-protection mechanism is named as the only risk the red team could not answer, and is therefore the highest-priority build in the company.
Technical integrity signals never auto-fail an assessment, because in this operating environment that punishes circumstance rather than conduct.

5. Session protocol
Open by reading this ledger and the decision register. Do not re-derive settled positions.
State the objective of the session and what will exist at the end of it.
Build completely, to the standing instructions. No outlines.
Verify: render every document, recalculate every workbook, check values are right rather than merely error-free.
Record decisions in K-00-02 and update this ledger.
File to the directory. Nothing is authoritative until it is filed.

End of document. Updated at every session.
