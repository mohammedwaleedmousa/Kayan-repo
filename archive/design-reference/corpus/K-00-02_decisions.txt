1. How this register works
Three states only. TAKEN means the decision is settled and downstream work may rely on it. PROPOSED means a position has been reasoned and recommended but awaits founder ratification. OPEN means the question is live and materially affects downstream architecture.
A decision may be reversed at any time, but only by adding a superseding entry with a reason. Silent reversal is the failure this register prevents, and it is the commonest way a company of this kind loses coherence over a year of fast work.

Q-02
Is the taxonomy rebuilt on the widened twenty-family structure, or does the earlier twelve-family, 441-node registry carry forward?
Registration navigation, assessment mapping, pod capability, all reporting. Highest blocking value.
Adopt the twenty-family structure. The earlier one excluded trades, hospitality and agriculture, which contradicts the inclusion principle.
Q-01
Is the public verification reference released at launch, or held until a delivery record exists?
Public profile design, external messaging, talent expectations.
Hold it. The mark earns meaning through delivery evidence; publishing an empty credential devalues it permanently.
Q-10
Is registration open to all at launch, or invitation-led for the first cohort?
Verification throughput, queue risk, launch reputation.
Invitation-led. A verification backlog is a reputational event, not an administrative one.
Q-06
Numeric thresholds: engagements required for T3, mentoring for T4, assessment pass bands.
Tier decisions cannot be made without them.
Set provisionally now, calibrate against the first hundred assessments.
Q-08
Does Forge completion substitute for part of the T2 assessment, or count as evidence alongside it?
Forge&apos;s value proposition to participants and to sponsors.
Evidence alongside, never substitution. Substitution would make the credential a function of who paid for training.
Q-03
Is the professional side ever a paying customer, or permanently free at entry?
Platform build, fairness argument, revenue model.
Permanently free at entry. Charging the supply side of a trust business inverts the incentive.
Q-07
Is a practical task required at T2 for all specialisations or only where a portfolio is inconclusive?
Assessment build volume and verification throughput.
Only where the portfolio is inconclusive. Otherwise throughput cannot meet demand.
Q-09
Cooling period between assessment attempts.
Assessment integrity design.
Set at a fixed interval; calibrate on observed retake behaviour.
Q-04
Fiscal year end.
Financial calendar, reporting cycle, audit planning.
Founder decision — no strong technical argument either way.
Q-05
Client concentration threshold triggering mandatory mitigation.
Sales strategy and risk policy.
Set a stated percentage of trailing twelve-month revenue.
