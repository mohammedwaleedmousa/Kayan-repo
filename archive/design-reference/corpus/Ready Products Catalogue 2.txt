01 IDEA
Choose. Fund. Start.
In a market where the hardest part of buying a service is trusting it will arrive, Kayan sells the trust pre-packaged.
In Aden today, commissioning professional work is an act of faith. Scope is argued over, prices are invented on the spot, deadlines slip, and there is rarely any recourse if the work never comes. The Ready Products Catalogue removes that friction entirely. Each product has a fixed scope, a fixed price, a named delivery date, and a managed team behind it — and every order is protected by escrow from the first day. The client does not negotiate a brief; they choose a product the way they would choose anything else they trust. This catalogue spans thirty-five products, from a USD 280 voice-over to a USD 5,500 custom platform, so that whoever walks through the door — an individual, a founder, a café, a hotel, an NGO, or a businessman — finds something built precisely for them.

06 MONEY
Where the Money Goes
Because the products are productised, the economics are transparent — and the same on every order. The client funds the full price into escrow at the Tadamon Islamic Bank before work begins. That money is never Kayan's revenue; it is the talent's, held safe until the work is accepted. On acceptance, the talent is paid 80%, Kayan recognises only its 20% net fee, and a 2% holdback is kept for 30 days against any defect, then released.

07 RULES
The Rules of the Catalogue
◆ Choose a tier where one exists. Several products (Website, Brand Identity, Social Media, Startup-in-a-Box, Market Study) come in Good / Better / Best tiers so a client can match the scope to the budget.
◆ One free revision is included unless the product states more. Further revisions are 20% of the product price.
◆ Acceptance is a 5-business-day window. Acceptance releases payment; silence within the window is deemed acceptance.
◆ Prices are in USD and held firm; payment in YER converts at the actual Aden rate on the day, recorded against the dated rate table — never a notional rate.
◆ Retainers are billed monthly in advance; rush delivery is +10% (it accelerates the pod, never skips a gate); and the founding rule is absolute — no signed order and no funded escrow means no work.
A product is the beginning of a relationship, not the end of a transaction: a Brand Identity leads to a Website and a Bookkeeping retainer; a Market Study leads to a Pitch Pack, an MVP, and a raise; a Restaurant Launch Kit grows into the Enterprise Suite. The catalogue is the front door; the managed-delivery engine is the house.
