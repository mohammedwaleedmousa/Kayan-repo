How we run · OPERATING COMMITMENTS

We are powered by the sun — and that is policy, not decoration.

The array on the roof is the visible half of an operating policy: how this floor treats energy, waste, and — above all — who gets to work here. Three commitments, written the way we write everything: measurable, and audited like the accounts.

01 · ENVIRONMENT
Sun first, waste last

The working day runs on solar; batteries carry the evening; the grid is backup, never the base. The café and floor are phasing out single-use plastic — glass, ceramic and refill by default — and paper is the exception: records live in the ledger, not the drawer.

02 · WOMEN AT THE CENTRE
A 60% floor, not a ceiling

Kayan commits to a minimum of 60% women across its task force — measured monthly, published with the audit, and carried into every cohort and pod we form. Not a pledge for the brochure; a number on the books.

03 · INCLUSION BY DESIGN
Every ability, one door

A step-free ground floor was a choice, not a coincidence. People of all abilities are not accommodated here — they are expected: in the commons, in the cohorts, and on the payroll. Capability has no single body.

These commitments sit in Kayan's operating policy alongside the escrow and verification rules — same register, same monthly audit, same consequences when missed.
