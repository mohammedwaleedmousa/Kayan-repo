Conditions · CONDITIONS

Conditions are published as they are, outages included

Power and connectivity are not givens in Aden. Anyone claiming they never fail is either lying or has not worked here. This panel shows the actual state from the operations source, and a field stays empty until the number arrives.

POWER
Solar with batteries

The floor runs on the sun first; the public grid is backup. Remaining reserve hours are published live.

The rules · HOUSE RULES

A place governed by rules written on its door

01

Entry by card

Tier one and above. A visitor enters on an invitation recorded in the name of whoever invited them.

02

The women's section is closed

No one passes through it and no route crosses it. This is an operating condition, not an added service.

03

No photography on the floor

Only with written consent from everyone in frame. No one's image is published for marketing.

04

The café is an amenity

It serves the floor and is not accounted for as a line of business; the Hub's success is not measured by its sales.

05

No partisan activity

And no event for a party to a conflict. The place is neutral by its regulation, not by intention.

06

Hours are published

On the door and on this page, and every unplanned closure is recorded with its cause.
