CHAPTER 01

The problem we remove

Five questions stop money from reaching capable hands. This line exists to answer all five inside one accountable institution.
A buyer in Riyadh, London, Nairobi or Aden has work that needs doing and a budget to pay for it. Between that budget and a capable Yemeni professional stand five unanswered questions. Platforms answer some and drop the rest; each dropped question kills deals silently.
RISK 1 · IDENTITY
Is this person real?
No verifiable identity layer exists for independent professionals here. A CV is a claim; we hold a checked record.
RISK 2 · COMPETENCE
Can they do this exact thing?
Not talent in general — the specific work you need. We assess against a named micro-specialisation, not a job title.
RISK 3 · CONTRACT
Does the agreement mean anything?
Cross-border enforcement into Yemen is impractical. So you contract with Kayan — one accountable counterparty, written scope, arbitration that works.
RISK 4 · PAYMENT
Who holds the money?
Pay first and hope, or deliver first and hope. We remove hope: funds sit in a protected account and release only on your acceptance.
RISK 5 · RECOURSE
Who answers if it fails?
A named institution with a dispute path measured in days — not a marketplace shrug. If delivery fails, that is Kayan's problem before it is yours.
WHY THIS IS A BUSINESS, NOT A CHARITY
The buyer does not pay us for hours. The buyer pays us for the five risks we take off their desk — and risk transfer is priced, tiered, and disclosed.
That is why the fee is a function of the promise level, why contributor pay is set independently and disclosed, and why the delivery record — not the marketing — is the asset that compounds. Skill was never the constraint here. Structure was.

Ten gates from enquiry to close. No gate opens before the one behind it is shut, and two never bend for anyone.

THE MONEY PATH
Client funds → protected account (a liability on our books, never revenue) → verified at G2 → frozen instantly if a dispute opens → released at G8 on two signatures → contributors paid with proof → Kayan books its net fee only. The ledger reconciles daily; a variance above one dollar is investigated within twenty-four hours.
WHEN IT GOES WRONG
A dispute freezes the milestone's funds the hour it is raised, then runs three levels in fourteen days: direct resolution, operations mediation, then a binding decision with an external adviser — recorded as precedent. Targets are published: under 5% of projects, four days average, satisfaction above 70%. Refunds follow the staged ladder; accepted work is never refundable; no contributor is docked without documented evidence and a right of reply.

CHAPTER 04

Who does the work

Pods, not freelancers. Named specialisations, not job titles. A ladder people climb, and a first rung anyone can reach.

The pod — the unit we sell

Kayan never sells an individual. It sells a pod: a small team with one accountable lead, composed for the scope, priced as a unit. The pod is why the fee defends itself, why delivery survives an absence — and absence here is a certainty, not a risk — and why an apprentice can sit safely inside real commercial work.
Lead
Owns delivery, speaks to the client, raises submissions, escalates early. Requires a delivery-proven tier and a track record. First name on the letter, first call in a storm.
Specialists
Verified in the exact node of the work — assessed capability, not claimed familiarity. Paid the disclosed role rate on time, every time.
Reviewer
Independent of execution, at least the tier of those reviewed. The reason a client never sees a first draft.
Apprentice
A standing structural seat, not charity: supervised contribution whose output always passes a reviewer. This seat is how absorption actually happens — a first paid, recorded, referenced engagement.
Three ways a pod forms — equal standing
Assembled by Kayan for a scope from the verified map — the default. Ready Pods: two to six verified professionals who register as a standing team under one lead with a written internal split — the fastest route from registration to revenue, and each member's incentive to recruit the others. Composed: individuals plus members borrowed from registered pods, with the lending pod's recorded consent. Formation type never affects who gets work.
The intern desk — the line's own first rung
Beyond the apprentice seat inside pods, the line itself runs intern positions under the First Rung standard: a documentation steward who chases and assembles packs, a delivery desk assistant on gate logistics and minutes, a WhatsApp desk runner on first-response scripts, a QA runner who routes review items. Every internship is scoped, supervised, credentialled on the card — and counted in the absorption ratio when it converts to paid pod work.

Specialisation, not generality

Matching runs on the company taxonomy: twenty families of work — from software to skilled trades, hospitality to health — seventy-nine groups, four hundred eighty-three coded nodes, each permanent, bilingual, and only active once a verification method exists for it. A pod's capability map and its gaps are read in codes, so a scope can be staffed in minutes and a gap is a fact, not an opinion.
SW-FE · React application build DE-BR · Brand identity design LN-TR · AR↔EN document translation RS-FD · Field data collection FN-MD · Financial modelling CM-RP · Report production MK-SM · Social media management CR-EL · Solar & electrical service HP-EV · Event operations AD-DG · Document digitisation
Illustrative nodes — the governed register carries the full four hundred eighty-three. A professional declares one primary node and up to four secondary; a plain-language route ("I fix air conditioners", "I translate documents") reaches the right node in three steps, because formal vocabulary as the only door quietly excludes exactly the people this institution exists to include.

CHAPTER 05

The Kayan Card

The card points; the record proves. What it carries, what it counts, and what it will never show.
FACE — WHO STANDS HERE
Name in both scripts, the public verification reference, the tier named in Arabic with its colour, the primary specialisation, the verification link. One vertical format, five tier states; the face engraving densifies as the record grows — a card you can read across a desk and trust across a border.
BACK — WHAT HAS BEEN DONE
The delivery strip: one mark per accepted engagement, and it grows only on an acceptance certificate — never by self-report. Volunteering credentialled through Kayan for Yemen is recorded as service marks. The Founding 100 carry the founding mark. Counts, dates, validity — nothing decorative.
THE THIRTY-SECOND CHECK
Any client, anywhere, opens the card's link on an average phone on a weak network: the public record loads without login, matches the person, states the tier and its validity, lists accepted deliveries. If display fails, a reasoned decision states what was not confirmed and the path to fix it. The card is an antenna; the register is the ground truth.
NEVER ON THE CARDWHY
Internal identifiersThe public reference reveals no volume or sequence; internal codes never leave the register.
Personal dataNo address, no age, no family status, no category labels of any kind — a person is described by what they do.
Anything unearnedNo aspirational badges, no purchased marks. A mark that cannot be revoked is decoration, and decoration is what this card exists to replace.

CHAPTER 06

One estate, one flywheel

What this line draws from the other three today — and what it must accumulate for the lines that are designed but deliberately not yet built.
With the Hub & Café
The Hub is this line's venue and witness: identity checks happen in person, pods hold work sessions at its tables, clients meet the institution over its coffee, and Hub members are the warmest talent and referral pool. In return the line fills the Hub with working teams and every documentation pack fills its walls with proof.
With the Forge
The Forge manufactures this line's supply at zero acquisition cost — sponsor-funded cohorts whose trainees work inside real pods during formation, in the apprentice seat, output always reviewed. The line returns the favour with the evidence sponsors buy: absorption computed from acceptance records, not attendance sheets.
With Kayan for Yemen
K4Y is the purpose running through this line, under its own mark: credentialled volunteering is many people's first structured work, recorded on the card; the volunteer-to-apprentice path feeds the intern desk; and the line's dignity rules — fair disclosed rates, on-time pay, evidence-settled disputes — are K4Y made operational, not a poster on the wall.
DESIGNED · GATED · NOT YET BUILT
E-working space
Digital membership at scale. Gate: the physical Hub above 60% capacity. This line's contribution: working pods that make remote membership worth buying.
Kayan Broadcast
The voice of the work — films, series and stories cut from consented documentation packs. Gate: a proof library deep enough to programme from. Every clean close in this line is raw material banked for it.
Kayan Ventures
Patient capital on top of the payment flow and delivery data. Gate: Phase 2 stable, treasury proven. This line is quietly writing its underwriting book: who delivers, at what quality, under what pressure.
The discipline is the point: these may be designed and even pre-sold, but no future line consumes build energy or capital before its published gate — trying every good idea at once is the classic death of a company like this.

The sales motion — seven steps, no dead ends
Enquiry lands (WhatsApp or the portal — two-minute response standard either way) → qualification against the three questions (need, authority, conversation) → a scoping call books within two days → SOW and funding note issue together → gates run with the client seeing G6 and G7 outputs → acceptance converts to a case within a week → the warranty-close letter carries the referral ask. Every enquiry exits as client, nurture, or a recorded referral to a fitter provider — never as silence.
Channel jobs
WhatsApp + Google Business convert — the only call to action anywhere. LinkedIn is the flagship: founder voice, cases, institutional credibility. Instagram/Facebook carry reach and the visual story — the real Hub, real faces with consent. X threads the model for the development community; Telegram reaches the diaspora. WhatsApp is the door; the portal is the house.
Feed rules — the line's four running formats
Proof Friday: one delivered project in submission-letter format. Faces of the work: one professional, named skill, consented photo. How your money is protected: the structure explained plainly. Product spotlight: one ready product, window and exclusions honest. Cadence 2 LinkedIn + 1 Instagram + 1 WhatsApp weekly; client language only — never internal jargon; no post ships without its logged objective and a ready next action.
The client portal — where the relationship lives
KAYANWORK.COM · SIGNED-IN HOME
WhatsApp opens the conversation; the portal is where it grows into a relationship. One signed-in home on the site where a client sees everything they have with Kayan — and where a client becomes a sponsor without a single meeting.
The engagement room
Each engagement as a live page: the gate it stands at, the scope line by line, files, minutes, the submission letter — and acceptance given with one signature-grade click that generates the certificate.
The money view
Protected balance, funded milestones, releases with dates and receipts, the holdback clock — the escrow ledger made client-visible, so the auditor's question answers itself.
The catalogue counter
The twelve ready products ordered directly: choose, see the written scope and exclusions, fund, start — repeat orders in three clicks, quotes for custom scopes requested from the same desk.
The sponsor console
Back a Forge cohort or a Kayan for Yemen campaign from the same account: pick the programme, see exactly what the money produces, and receive reports generated from acceptance records — never hand-assembled.
Verification lookup
Any card's public reference resolves to its record — tier, validity, accepted deliveries — no login needed, weak-network tolerant.
Build order
At launch: engagement room + money view + lookup, generated from the registers (never hand-maintained). Then the catalogue counter, then the sponsor console. WhatsApp remains the always-on fallback — Aden's connectivity votes on architecture.
