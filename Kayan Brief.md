## What Kayan is
One institution, three things in one arc: **verifies people before the work, manages + guarantees delivery during it, stands behind the outcome after.** Trust infrastructure — not a marketplace, agency, platform, workspace, training provider, or NGO. Category: impact sourcing for professional services in a conflict market, with a guarantee no incumbent offers. Founding rule: **no signed scope + no funded guarantee → no work begins.**

## Architecture: 3 lines + 1 civic arm (+ enablers)
- **Managed Delivery** — the engine. Commissioned work via verified pods, escrow-protected, net-fee.
- **The Hub** — working floor on the Corniche; solar, always-on, free fast internet, women's safe space, accessible; café is an amenity, not a business line.
- **Kayan Forge** — trains, assesses, verifies; sponsor-funded cohorts (never "Academy" — name prohibited).

- Flywheel: Forge feeds bench → bench delivers → work funds Forge → Hub houses all → K4Y feeds tomorrow's bench.
