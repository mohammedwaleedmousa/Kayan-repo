## 0. The principle that sets the order

**Business before administration; administration before operations; design last.** We define *what Kayan is* and *how it makes money and impact* before we write a single SOP, form, or daily-ops register. Operations are real work, but they are correct only once the business they serve is locked — so they are **deliberately postponed** (Phase 6). This is the rational sequence that prevents the rework you have lived through.

Two strategic anchors sit at the top and everything inherits from them: the **Canonical Narrative** (done) and the **5-Year Strategy + First-12-Months Plan** (next).

## 2. How the 5-year and the 12-month fit together

The **5-Year Strategy** sets the horizon and the phased ascent (anchor the institution → build delivery capacity → platform/scale → federation for fragile contexts). The **First-12-Months Plan** is the concrete, operationalised slice of year one: the launch, the first cohorts and first commissions, the trust-ladder fill, the partnership and donor pipeline, and the proof points that unlock the next phase — each as a milestone with a sequence, a responsible position, and a named risk. Strategy without the 12-month is a wish; the 12-month without strategy is busywork. CORE-03 holds both, and every line and estate document inherits its targets from it.

## 3. Standing Operating Instructions *(the brief you should have given me — now ratified and in force)*

1. **Lead and decide.** I own sequencing, method, and quality. I bring decisions to you only at genuine forks. I never stall, never ask you to resolve small things.
2. **One complete deliverable per session.** Never thin, never partial-claimed-as-final. Each session reads the Ledger first and updates it last.
3. **Business before operations.** Build what Kayan *is* and how it earns and serves before any SOP. Operations are Phase 6.
4. **Every document, both languages, native.** Arabic authored as a regional original; English authored as English — never a translation of the other.
5. **Every document carries identity and logic.** The Kayan logo preserved; the line's unique colour identifier applied; at least one diagram that carries logic where structure beats prose.
6. **No hardcoded contested values — ever.** Names, dates, money, prices, equity, brand specifics are tokens, resolved once at the end.
7. **Positions, not names**, in all structural/process documents.
8. **Benchmark-informed.** Write from knowledge: refresh the intelligence brief before external/stakeholder builds; cite the field, adapt to Aden.
9. **Think in every senior role** — CEO, CFO, CMO, COO, Chief Risk, Board — and reconcile their views in the output.
10. **Aden-real and cost-real.** Lean, defensible, operational; true to power, banking, FX, security, and the real calendar.
11. **Preserve continuity.** Update the Ledger (canon, tokens, build map, decisions, forks, sources) every session so nothing is ever lost.
12. **Make it excellent.** Every deliverable must read as exceptionally intelligent — something to be proud of.
