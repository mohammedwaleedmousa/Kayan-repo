What the client buys · FIVE RISKS REMOVED

Nobody buys work. What is bought is five risks removed.

01 · IDENTITY
Identity

Who works is known by name and by document, and verified before award, not after.

02 · COMPETENCE
Competence

Skill is measured by a practical task and a reviewer's judgement, not by a claim in a CV.

03 · CONTRACT
Contract

A framework agreement, a numbered scope of work and a written acceptance standard, all signed before the first hour.

04 · PAYMENT
Payment

Funds are held in escrow and released only against a signed certificate and dual authorisation.

05 · ACCOUNTABILITY
Accountability

One party answers and the decision issues from it. The client is never passed between individuals.

The gates · TEN GATES, THREE PHASES

Ten gates. Each one has a deliverable and a named owner.

No gate opens before the one before it closes. Every gate decision is recorded with its owner, its date and its reason, and appears in the client's engagement file exactly as the institution sees it.

Three review layers

The executor, then a peer who did not execute, then an independent reviewer. The three never meet in one person.

Deemed acceptance

Five working days of client silence after delivery counts as acceptance, and is recorded as such in the certificate.

Escalation

If a milestone drifts at the mid-point review, a Tier 4 lead is assigned to the team before the delivery gate.

Service tiers · S1 · S2 · S3

No single rate across every engagement. The tier is fixed before signature.
S1 · ESSENTIAL Default
Essential

Verified people, a written scope, an escrow account and one coordinator. Not offered first. Offered when a client insists.

Institution fee 25–30%
S2 · ASSURED Default
Assured

Adds a named team lead, an independent quality review, and a written acceptance test both sides are held to.

Institution fee 35–42%
S3 · GUARANTEED Default
Guaranteed

Fixed price, a date binding in the contract, executor replacement at the institution's cost, and defects liability after acceptance.

Institution fee 45–55%
The margin on the guaranteed tier comes from the warranty reserve when it is released unclaimed. The economics of this tier rest on verification actually working, not on verification being promised. The percentages above are planning ranges from the economic architecture; the final price issues from the approved price card.
The outcome model · BUY THE RESULT

You buy the outcome, not the hours.

The client names the result and its date, and signs its acceptance at the end. Everything between those two signatures — the pod, the quality, the risk — is Kayan's to carry, alone.

THE CLIENT HOLDS
What the client holds

A written price that does not move, a named date, an acceptance gate only the client signs, and a thirty-day warranty after handover. Funds sit in escrow, untouched until acceptance.

KAYAN CARRIES
What Kayan carries

An executor falters — replaced at Kayan's cost, not the client's. An estimate proves wrong — Kayan absorbs the difference; the price is never reopened. Independent review runs before the client ever sees the work. That is the model: risk is eaten here, not passed along.

THE TALENT RECEIVES
What the talent receives

A rate set independently of the client's price, a named role on the acceptance record, payout on acceptance — not when cash flow allows — and a ledger entry that compounds reputation, engagement after engagement.

The price and its floor · ONE QUOTE · ONE FLOOR

One written quote. One honest floor — held even when it costs us the request.

01 · THE QUOTE
One written quote

The full scope is priced before the first hour of work and funded into escrow. No hourly meter, no line that appears after signature.

02 · WHAT IT BUYS
What the price buys

A verified pod with named roles, independent review by someone who did not execute, two revision rounds included, a 2% warranty held for thirty days, and a complete documentation pack.

03 · WHY A FLOOR
Why a floor at all

Below the floor the work does not get cheaper — someone pays the difference: an executor short-paid, or a review cut. Requests below it are declined with a reasoned decision, and a smaller scope at an honest price is always offered instead.

The sector is recorded at qualification. It is fixed in the file and is not revised later to justify a price.
Cross-subsidy is declared. Small Yemeni firms are served at a lower coefficient. The difference is carried by the higher sectors, and published monthly.
The executor's fee is independent. It is not derived from the client price, and it is disclosed to both sides.
The pod · THREE POD TYPES · FOUR ROLES

Three kinds of team. None ranks above another.

TYPE A
Assembled for the engagement

Drawn from the register for this engagement, and dissolved at close.

TYPE B
Pre-registered team

Two to six verified professionals bound to one another before the work. They register under their own name, keep their own shares, and are held to account as one unit.

TYPE C
Composed by the institution

Kayan composes it from the register when no standing team matches the request.

Lead

One, and no more. Holds the decision inside the scope, signs milestone deliverables, and answers for delay by name.

Executor

Executes within their discipline. Deliverables are recorded under their name in the register.

Reviewer

Did not execute what they review. Execution and review never sit with the same person.

Trainee

The trainee seat is the declared absorption mechanism. It is how a newcomer crosses into paid, documented work rather than observation.

Team bonus, 2%. Paid from the institution fee on a close with no reservation and no warranty claim, split on the same team shares.
Documentation duty, 5%. Held back from the final release until the documentation pack is complete, within seven days of the quality gate.
Team disputes go to the lead. Then to the delivery desk. An internal disagreement never reaches the client.
The money path · TWO RAILS · FOUR RELEASE CONDITIONS

Collected on one rail. Distributed on another.

RAIL A · COLLECTION
Collection and holding

Client funds are collected and held outside Yemen, in a jurisdiction international bodies can pay into without friction. They are booked as a liability, not as revenue.

The jurisdiction and the transfer channel are an open decision, and a precondition to launch.
RAIL B · DISTRIBUTION
Internal distribution

Executors inside Yemen are paid through Tadhamon International Islamic Bank in Aden and channels controlled with it. Cash is an exception, capped, and requires dual authorisation.

No informal transfer, no cryptocurrency, no personal accounts. The base currency is the dollar; reporting runs in rial at a rate fixed by date in each contract.
