Five rules we wrote before we took our first commission:

1. Money before movement — your full amount banked, untouched, before work starts.
2. Scope is a document, not a memory — nothing verbal binds either of us.
3. Only verified hands touch client work — tested, proven, in the exact skill.
4. Quality is inspected three times, by three different people — the third one
   is independent and paid by us, never by your money.
5. Silence has a meaning, and it's written down — five days to review; anything
   missing from the written scope, we fix free.

We built these rules because this market has been burned by their absence.
They cost us speed sometimes. They buy you certainty always.

Behind every outcome, a Kayan.
