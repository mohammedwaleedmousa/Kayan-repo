# Pod playbook — Brand Identity Pack
*(RP-001 · internal — how the pod delivers, step by step)*

## The pod
The pod (lead = Designer):
  - Designer (T2+): 22 hours
  - QA (—): 2 hours
The Designer leads: owns the client relationship, the direction, and final sign-off.

## The milestones
- **D1** — Kickoff + discovery · client: 45-min discovery call · evidence: Discovery notes
- **D4** — Two directions · client: Client picks + 20-min call · evidence: Two logo directions
- **D9** — Developed system · client: Checkpoint message · evidence: Full brand system
- **D12** — QA layer-3 · client: — · evidence: QA sign-off
- **D13** — Submission letter · client: Client receives pack · evidence: Submission letter
- **+5 days** — Acceptance window · client: 5-day silence = acceptance · evidence: Acceptance / revision request

## Definition of done
Done when every acceptance line is met and independent QA has signed off at 4.0+. The
logo opens correctly in every format, the guide covers usage and misuse, and the social
templates are editable. Nothing ships in the submission letter until then.

## The taste-dispute guard
Taste disputes are pre-empted by making the Day-4 direction choice the client's own,
documented. Further changes beyond the included revision are a warm change order.

## Escalation
Quality or deadline at risk → the Designer escalates to the MD Lead. Funds for any
undelivered milestone are never released.
