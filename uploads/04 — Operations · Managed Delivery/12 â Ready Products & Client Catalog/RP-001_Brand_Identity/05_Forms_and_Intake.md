## The brief (built from the intake)
The Designer turns the intake into a one-page creative brief: what the brand must say,
the must-haves and must-avoids, where it will be used, and the milestones. Reviewed with
the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
Attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work stating: the fixed price
($500), the 14-day timeline, the deliverables (logo suite, palette,
type, 12-page guide, stationery, social kit), the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one
included revision round, and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work; funds at G3.
