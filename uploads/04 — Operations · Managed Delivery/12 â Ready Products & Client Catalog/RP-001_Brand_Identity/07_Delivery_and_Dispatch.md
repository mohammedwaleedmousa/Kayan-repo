# Delivery & dispatch — Brand Identity Pack
*(RP-001 · handover, submission, acceptance, warranty, money release)*

## The handover pack
- The source files (AI/SVG/PNG) and the font licence notes
- The 12-page brand guide (PDF)
- The stationery and social kit, editable
- A 15-minute usage call so the client can run the brand

## The submission letter
Issued at the final milestone. States what is delivered, confirms each acceptance line is
met, and opens the 5-day acceptance window. Sent through the client's Kayan portal.

## Acceptance
The client accepts by confirming, or by five days of silence after the submission letter
(G7). Revenue is recognised at acceptance.

## Warranty & money release
A 2% quality holdback is released 30 days after acceptance (G8/G9). Undelivered milestones
are never paid.

## The ask (turn delivery into the next thing)
On acceptance, the Designer closes warmly and — with consent — invites a testimonial
and flags the natural next products: Professional Website, Content Retainer, Corporate Documents Pack.
