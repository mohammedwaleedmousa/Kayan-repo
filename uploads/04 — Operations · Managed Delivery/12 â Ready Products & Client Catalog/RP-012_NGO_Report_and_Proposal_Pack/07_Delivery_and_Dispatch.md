# Delivery & dispatch — NGO Report & Proposal Pack
*(RP-012 · handover, submission, acceptance, warranty, money release)*

## The handover pack
- The final deliverables (editable files + PDF as ordered)
- The QA sign-off
- The evidence-reference index
- A short plain-language guide to using what's delivered

## The submission letter
Issued at the final milestone. It states what is delivered, confirms each acceptance line
is met, and opens the 5-day acceptance window. Sent through the client's Kayan portal.

## Acceptance
The client accepts by confirming, or by five days of silence after the submission letter
(G7). Revenue is recognised at acceptance.

## Warranty & money release
A 2% quality holdback is released 30 days after acceptance (G8/G9), covering any defect
raised in that window. Undelivered milestones are never paid.

## The ask (turn delivery into the next thing)
On acceptance, the Writer closes warmly and — with consent — invites a testimonial
and flags the natural next products: M&E Study, Data Collection & Dashboard, Annual Report Design + Write.
