# RP-012 · NGO REPORT & PROPOSAL PACK — TOR
Family: Content, Writing & Translation · Pod: Writer T3+ · Designer · Translator · Delivery: 12 days

1 PROMISE: Donor-grade paper in 12 days — a results report or proposal written to the logframe, designed for reading, and translated faithfully — by a verified trio that knows what an audit asks.
2 SCOPE IN: structure workshop against donor template/logframe · full write-up (report ≤40pp or proposal per call requirements) · results tables with evidence references · design & infographics (accessible) · AR/EN version · compliance checklist pass against the donor's own guidelines
  NOT: M&E data collection (RP-013/014) · budget construction (RP-026 add-on) · submission portal handling (client signs & submits)
3 PRICE & TERMS: PRICE ◇ set in the consolidated figures pass · pod paid via the 80/20/2 split at gates · 1 free revision round on the agreed direction, further rounds +20% · rush +10% · funds 100% guaranteed at G3 before work starts · revenue only at G7 acceptance (5-day silence = acceptance) · 2% quality holdback released +30 days (G8/G9).
4 POD: Writer T3+ (24h) · Designer (10h) · Translator (12h). Lead = writer.
5 MILESTONES: D1 structure+sources lock · D5 full draft (client 48h review) · D8 design+translation · D10 compliance checklist + QA · D11 submission letter · +5-day window
6 ACCEPTANCE: every claimed result carries an evidence reference · donor formatting rules met line-by-line (checklist attached) · AR/EN semantic parity confirmed by reviewer
7 HANDOVER: editable + PDF, checklist, evidence-reference index
8 RISKS: evidence gaps discovered mid-write → flagged day-found, never papered over (the K4Y honesty clause applies to client work too) · deadline compression → rush rule, stated at G0
9 LADDER: → RP-014 M&E Study · RP-013 Dashboard · RP-018 Annual Report
STANDARDS & INCLUSION: Results language aligned to OECD-DAC criteria vocabulary where the donor uses it; disaggregation shown where data exists.
