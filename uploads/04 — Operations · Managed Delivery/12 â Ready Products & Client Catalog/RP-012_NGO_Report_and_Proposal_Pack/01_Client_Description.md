# NGO Report & Proposal Pack — what you get
*(RP-012 · client-facing description for kayanwork.com)*

## Donor-grade paper in 12 days

A donor-grade report or proposal in twelve days — written to your logframe or the donor's template, every result carrying its evidence, designed to be read, and delivered in both Arabic and English. Built by a verified writer, designer, and translator who know exactly what an audit looks for, with your payment protected until you accept the work.

## What arrives
- A full write-up — a results report (up to 40 pages) or a proposal built to the call's requirements
- Results tables where every claimed result carries an evidence reference
- Clean, accessible design and infographics that make the paper easy to read and to trust
- A faithful Arabic and English version, checked for meaning, not just words
- A compliance pass against the donor's own formatting and content rules, line by line

## How it works
1. **Day 1** — Structure workshop — we lock the outline against your donor template or logframe, and agree the evidence sources
2. **Day 5** — Full draft delivered — you have 48 hours to review and comment
3. **Day 8** — Design and translation — the paper is laid out, infographics built, and the Arabic/English versions produced
4. **Day 10** — Compliance checklist and independent quality check against the donor's own guidelines
5. **Day 11** — Submission letter — the finished pack is handed to you
6. **+5 days** — Your acceptance window — we're not done until it's right

## The guarantee

accept the work. One free revision round on the agreed direction is included.

## Common questions
**Do you write to a specific donor's template?**
Yes — we build to your donor's template or logframe from day one, and pass a compliance checklist against their own guidelines before delivery.

**What if the evidence for a result isn't there?**
We flag it the day we find it, and never paper over a gap. Honest reporting protects you in an audit — it's the same standard we hold ourselves to.

**Can you also build the budget?**
The budget is a separate piece — we can add our Financial Model product to construct it alongside.

**Who owns the final files?**
You do, fully — editable files and PDF, plus an evidence-reference index you can reuse.
