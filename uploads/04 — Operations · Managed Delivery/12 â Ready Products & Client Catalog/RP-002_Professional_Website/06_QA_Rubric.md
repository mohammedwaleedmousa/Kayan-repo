# QA rubric — Professional Website
*(RP-002 · layer-3 QA · the exact lines the submission letter checks)*

Independent QA reviewer (not a member of the delivery pod). Score each area ≥ 4.0 to pass.

## Acceptance criteria (the client's promise)
- [ ] All 7 pages are present per the agreed sitemap
- [ ] The site is mobile-clean on real devices
- [ ] You successfully edit a page live during the training
- [ ] Forms deliver to your stated inbox or WhatsApp
- [ ] The SSL padlock is green

## Quality layer
- [ ] The work meets the standard for technology, software & digital — clear, correct, and usable
- [ ] Every claim, figure, or result carries its source or evidence (the house rule)
- [ ] Scope matches the SOW exactly — nothing silently added or dropped
- [ ] Arabic/English parity confirmed where both are delivered
- [ ] Built on the Kayan design-system method; mobile-first; bilingual-ready; hosting owned in the client's name — never locked to Kayan.

## The gate
If any line scores below 4.0, the work returns to the pod with specific notes before the
submission letter is issued. QA sign-off is attached to the handover.
