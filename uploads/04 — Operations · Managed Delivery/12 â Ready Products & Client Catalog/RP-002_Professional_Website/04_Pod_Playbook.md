# Pod playbook — Professional Website
*(RP-002 · internal — how the pod delivers, step by step)*

## The pod
The pod (lead = Developer):
  - Developer (T2+): 30 hours
  - UI Designer (T2+): 14 hours
  - Writer (polish) (—): 4 hours
  - QA (—): 3 hours
The Developer leads: owns the client relationship, the scope, and the final sign-off.

## The milestones
- **D1** — Kickoff + sitemap · client: Client confirms sitemap · evidence: Signed sitemap
- **D5** — Design frame check-in · client: Client approves look · evidence: Design frame
- **D10** — Build demo link · client: Client sees real site · evidence: Demo URL
- **D16** — Content-in + QA · client: Client content delivered · evidence: QA sign-off
- **D18** — Submission · client: Client receives site · evidence: Submission letter
- **D21** — Training · client: Client edits live · evidence: Training + guide
- **+5 days** — Acceptance window · client: 5-day silence = acceptance · evidence: Acceptance / revision request

## Definition of done
The work is done when every acceptance line below is met and the independent QA layer has
signed off at 4.0 or above. Nothing goes to the client in the submission letter until then.

## The honesty clause
This product carries Kayan's honesty clause: any gap in evidence, data, or scope is flagged
the day it's found and never papered over. Honest delivery protects the client and Kayan —
it is the same standard Kayan applies to its own impact reporting.

## Escalation
If quality or deadline is at risk, the Developer escalates to the MD Lead immediately.
Funds for any undelivered milestone are never released.
