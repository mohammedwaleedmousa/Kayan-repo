# Intake & forms — Professional Website
*(RP-002 · the intake form + brief + SOW annex for this product)*

## The client intake form
Captured on the website when a client requests this product, or filled with them on a
scoping call. Every field feeds the SOW and the pod brief automatically.

**1. Your organisation**
   *What does your organisation do, and who is the site for?*   `[text]`

**2. The pages**
   *What pages do you need? (Home, About, Services, Contact… tell us.)*   `[text]`

**3. The look**
   *Do you have a brand, logo, and colours, or do you need those first?*   `[file]`

**4. Content**
   *Do you have text and images ready, or will you need help?*   `[select]`

**5. Contact & WhatsApp**
   *Which inbox and WhatsApp number should the forms and buttons connect to?*   `[text]`

**6. Language**
   *Arabic, English, or both?*   `[select]`

**7. The deadline**
   *When do you need the site live?*   `[date]`

## The brief (built from the intake)
The Developer turns the intake into a one-page pod brief: the goal, the scope in and out,
the milestones, the acceptance lines, and any flags. Reviewed with the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
This product attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work that states:
the fixed price ($1200), the 21-day timeline, the deliverables listed in
the client description, the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one included revision round,
and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work starts; funds confirmed at G3.
