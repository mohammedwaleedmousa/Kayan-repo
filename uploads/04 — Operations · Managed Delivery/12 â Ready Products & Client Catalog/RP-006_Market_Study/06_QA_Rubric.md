# QA rubric — Market Study
*(RP-006 · layer-3 QA · the exact lines the submission letter checks)*

Independent QA reviewer (not a member of the delivery pod). Score each area ≥ 4.0 to pass.

## Acceptance criteria (the client's promise)
- [ ] Every figure is sourced, or clearly labelled an estimate with its method shown
- [ ] Every scoped question is answered, or honestly bounded where the data won't stretch
- [ ] The one-page decision brief stands on its own and is genuinely actionable

## Quality layer
- [ ] The work meets the standard for research, data & evaluation — clear, correct, and usable
- [ ] Every claim, figure, or result carries its source or evidence (the house rule)
- [ ] Scope matches the SOW exactly — nothing silently added or dropped
- [ ] Arabic/English parity confirmed where both are delivered
- [ ] Findings disaggregated (sex, age, governorate) where the data permits; sources cited to a consistent standard; no figure without provenance — the house rule.

## The gate
If any line scores below 4.0, the work returns to the pod with specific notes before the
submission letter is issued. QA sign-off is attached to the handover.
