## Common questions
**Will the findings stay independent?**
Yes — an independence clause is written into the scope. The programme team is heard at validation and every comment is logged, but the conclusions remain the evaluator's.

**What framework do you use?**
The OECD-DAC evaluation criteria, with a matrix mapping your questions to the criteria and the methods used to answer each.

**What if the data is thin?**
We state confidence levels plainly and never inflate them. An honest evaluation is what a donor can actually lean on.

**What about sensitive topics?**
We work to do-no-harm ethics, informed consent, and a survivor-referral line where safeguarding topics surface.
