# RP-005 · PROFESSIONAL TRANSLATION — TOR
Family: Content, Writing & Translation · Pod: Translator T2+ · Reviewer · Delivery: 7 days

1 PROMISE: Faithful, publication-ready translation in 7 days — translated by a verified linguist and independently reviewed, terminology locked before work starts, money protected until you accept.
2 SCOPE IN: up to the word-count in the SOW (AR↔EN core; other pairs by scoping) · terminology sheet agreed at kickoff · full second-linguist review · formatted delivery mirroring the source layout
  NOT: certified/sworn legal attestation (scoped separately with counsel) · desktop publishing beyond source layout (add Designer) · transcreation of slogans (RP-003/016 territory)
3 PRICE & TERMS: PRICE ◇ set in the consolidated figures pass · pod paid via the 80/20/2 split at gates · 1 free revision round on the agreed direction, further rounds +20% · rush +10% · funds 100% guaranteed at G3 before work starts · revenue only at G7 acceptance (5-day silence = acceptance) · 2% quality holdback released +30 days (G8/G9).
4 POD: Translator T2+ (per 1k words, scoped) · Reviewer (40% of translation hours). Lead = translator.
5 MILESTONES: D1 kickoff+terminology lock · D4 draft complete · D5 independent review · D6 QA + submission letter · D7–11 five-day window
6 ACCEPTANCE: terminology sheet honoured throughout · no untranslated segments · reviewer sign-off attached · layout mirrors source
7 HANDOVER: final files (docx/pdf as ordered) + terminology sheet for reuse
8 RISKS: source ambiguity → query log answered by client, not guessed · dialect/register mismatch → register agreed at kickoff (formal/plain)
9 LADDER: → RP-007 Corporate Documents · RP-012 NGO Pack · RP-018 Annual Report
STANDARDS & INCLUSION: Practice informed by ISO 17100 principles (qualified translator + independent revision); plain-language versions available for public-facing texts.
