# Professional Translation — what you get
*(RP-005 · client-facing description for kayanwork.com)*

## Faithful, publication-ready translation in 7 days

A faithful, publication-ready translation in seven days — Arabic and English at the core, other language pairs by arrangement. Translated by a verified linguist, independently reviewed by a second, with the terminology agreed before a single word is translated and your payment protected until you accept the work.

## What arrives
- Your text translated up to the agreed word count, in the language pair you need
- A terminology sheet, agreed with you at kickoff and honoured throughout
- A full independent review by a second linguist
- Delivery formatted to mirror your source layout

## How it works
1. **Day 1** — Kickoff and terminology lock — we agree the key terms and the register before work starts
2. **Day 4** — Draft translation complete
3. **Day 5** — Independent review by a second linguist
4. **Day 6** — Quality check and submission letter
5. **+5 days** — Your acceptance window

## The guarantee
Your payment is protected in a supervised bank-held account and released only as you
accept the work. One free revision round on the agreed direction is included.

## Price
$350, fixed. (Rush delivery +10%. Additional revision rounds +20%.)

## What's not included
- Certified or sworn legal attestation (scoped separately with counsel)
- Desktop publishing beyond your source layout (add a Designer)
- Transcreation of slogans and taglines (that's the brand work)

## Common questions
**Which languages?**
Arabic and English are our core pair; other pairs are available by scoping. Tell us what you need.

**How do you keep terminology consistent?**
We agree a terminology sheet with you at kickoff and hold to it throughout — and you keep the sheet to reuse on future work.

**Is it really two people?**
Yes. A verified translator does the work, and a second independent linguist reviews it before it reaches you — informed by ISO 17100 principles.

**Can you certify it for legal use?**
Sworn or certified legal attestation is a separate service we scope with counsel — ask us.
