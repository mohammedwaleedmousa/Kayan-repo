# Intake & forms — Professional Translation
*(RP-005 · the intake form + brief + SOW annex for this product)*

## The client intake form
Captured on the website when a client requests this product, or filled with them on a
scoping call. Every field feeds the SOW and the pod brief automatically.

**1. The source text**
   *Upload the document(s) to be translated.*   `[file]`

**2. The language pair**
   *From which language, into which? (Arabic ↔ English is our core.)*   `[select]`

**3. The register**
   *Formal or plain language? Who is the audience?*   `[select]`

**4. Word count & deadline**
   *Roughly how many words, and when do you need it?*   `[text]`

**5. Terminology**
   *Any preferred terms, a glossary, or past translations we should match?*   `[file]`

**6. Layout**
   *Should we mirror the source layout, or is a plain document fine?*   `[select]`

## The brief (built from the intake)
The Translator turns the intake into a one-page pod brief: the goal, the scope in and out,
the milestones, the acceptance lines, and any flags. Reviewed with the client before G3.

## The SOW annex
This product attaches to the Master Services Agreement via a Scope of Work that states:
the fixed price ($350), the 7-day timeline, the deliverables listed in
the client description, the acceptance criteria (file 06), the one included revision round,
and the payment-protection terms. Signed before work starts; funds confirmed at G3.
