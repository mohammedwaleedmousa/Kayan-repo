1. How to use this document
This document answers one question: what is Kayan, precisely enough that a competent stranger could run it. It is not marketing, it is not a pitch, and it is not a plan. It is the fixed point from which plans are derived.
Three rules govern its use:
Nothing downstream may contradict it. A form, a contract, a price, a job description or a website page that conflicts with this document is wrong by definition and must be corrected.
It changes only by recorded decision. Amendments are logged in K-00-02 Decision Register with date, rationale and author. Silent drift is the failure mode this document exists to prevent.
Claims are labelled. Every material statement carries a label: FACT (evidenced and locked), JUDGEMENT (professional interpretation taken as a working position), or OPEN (a decision not yet taken). Nothing is asserted as settled that is not.
2. The problem Kayan exists to solve
The conventional account of the Yemeni labour market is that there is a shortage of opportunity for an abundance of talent. That account is true but useless, because it does not identify what is actually binding.
The binding constraint is not the supply of skill. Skill is abundant, and it is cheap relative to the quality it delivers. The binding constraint is that there is no mechanism to transmit a credible signal of that skill across a border to a buyer who has no way to verify it, and no mechanism to move money in either direction with confidence. [JUDGEMENT]
Consider a buyer — a company in Riyadh, a diaspora professional in London, a programme officer in an aid agency in Aden. They have work to be done. They cannot answer five questions:
Is this person real, and are they who they claim to be?
Are they actually competent at the specific thing I need, as opposed to generally capable?
If I contract with them, is that contract worth anything?
If I pay in advance, will I see the work? If I pay on delivery, will they trust me?
If it goes wrong, who is accountable, and what recourse do I have?
Existing institutions answer some of these and fail at the rest. Open marketplaces solve discovery and fail at assurance — they transfer all risk to the buyer and all fee pressure to the worker. Agencies solve assurance and fail at scale — they are opaque, capacity-capped, and dependent on the personal credibility of a principal. Training programmes build skill and fail at absorption — they produce certificates that no employer prices, and they measure themselves on people trained rather than people earning.
What is missing is an institution that manufactures trust as a product: that takes latent, unverifiable talent and converts it into contract-grade, insurable, deliverable professional capacity — and then stands behind it. [JUDGEMENT]
That is the entire thesis of Kayan.
3. What Kayan is
3.1 The category sentence
Kayan is the verified professional infrastructure of the frontier economy: the institution that establishes, evidences and stands behind the competence of professionals in constrained markets, so that their work can be bought with confidence anywhere in the world.
Two words in that sentence are load-bearing. Infrastructure, because Kayan is not a party to the work — it is the layer that makes the work transactable, and layers compound where services do not. Verified, because verification is the asset; everything else is delivery of that asset.

These are not values statements. They are testable commitments: every future decision may be checked against them, and a decision that violates one is presumptively wrong.

Skill has no geography. Circumstance is not capability.

5. The product
Kayan does not sell people, and it does not sell hours. It sells de-risked delivery: a defined outcome, produced by verified professionals, under a written scope, with the client&apos;s money held in escrow until the work is accepted.
The client is buying the removal of five risks — identity risk, competence risk, contract risk, payment risk and accountability risk. That is what the fee is for, and it is why the fee is defensible at a level a pure marketplace could never charge. [JUDGEMENT]

5.2 What the professional receives
The professional receives the thing they cannot manufacture alone: a credible, evidenced, third-party account of their competence, and access to buyers who would never otherwise have taken the risk. They also receive protection — a written engagement, a funded escrow, and a fee that is disclosed rather than extracted.

6.1 How the loop closes
The insight that makes Kayan financially unusual is this: sponsor and donor capital funds the supply side of a commercial business, and the commercial business produces the evidence that unlocks the next round of sponsor capital. [JUDGEMENT]
Sponsor or programme capital funds a Forge cohort or a K4Y programme.
Forge trains, assesses and verifies. Verified professionals enter the registry — at no acquisition cost to the commercial line.
Managed Delivery sells that verified capacity to clients and earns a net fee.
Delivery produces two outputs: revenue, and evidence — accepted deliverables, earnings records, and the Absorption Ratio.
That evidence is the strongest possible case to the next sponsor, and the strongest possible proof to the next client.
Each turn of the loop lowers talent acquisition cost, raises the value of the Kayan credential, and deepens the delivery record that makes the next sale easier.
The Hub sits at the centre of the loop as its physical anchor: it is where in-person verification happens, where Forge cohorts run, where pods work, where clients and sponsors are hosted, and where evidence is produced. It converts what would be a fixed overhead into a revenue line and a proof asset simultaneously. [JUDGEMENT]

8. Revenue architecture
8.1 The agent doctrine
Kayan recognises as revenue only its own fee. The gross value of a client project is not Kayan revenue; it is a flow that passes through Kayan, of which the client&apos;s portion payable to contributors is a liability until discharged. [FACT — accounting policy, locked]
This is not a presentational preference. It determines the balance sheet, the tax position, the investor narrative and the integrity of every KPI. A company that books gross flows as revenue is telling itself a lie that will be discovered by the first serious counterparty.

10. Operating rules that may not be broken
#
Rule
Why
R1
No engagement begins without a signed scope and funded escrow.
Removes the single largest cause of dispute and non-payment.
R2
Verification status never determines who receives work.
Work allocation is a separate decision based on fit, availability and client requirement. Conflating them corrupts the ladder and invites accusations of favouritism.
R3
Kayan revenue is the net fee only.
Integrity of every financial statement and KPI.
R4
Cash payments are an exception, pre-approved, documented, and capped monthly.
Cash is the primary vector for loss, fraud and untraceability in this operating environment.
R5
Every person, organisation and engagement has one record and one identifier.
Prevents the duplicate-record decay that destroys reporting.
R6
A human touches a record only at a genuine judgement gate.
Any process requiring routine manual handling breaks between 100 and 1,000 registrations.
R7
Any claim made externally must be traceable to an artefact.
Reputation in a trust business is the entire balance sheet.
R8
No personal data is collected without a stated purpose, a retention period and a specific consent.
Legal exposure, and the credibility of a company whose product is trust.
R9
Client concentration above a stated threshold triggers a documented mitigation plan.
A single client at 60% of revenue is not a business, it is a contract.
R10
Nothing is described as complete until it can be operated by someone who was not present when it was built.
The founder-dependency failure mode.

Residual
Verification has no willing payer
Nobody will pay for a badge. Verification is a cost centre dressed as a moat.
Verification is never sold standalone at launch. It is the input that makes managed delivery sellable at an agency-grade fee, and the outcome that makes sponsor capital fundable. It is monetised twice, indirectly.
Low. But we must never build a P&L line that depends on selling verification directly before Phase 2.

High and unresolved until the escrow and payout mechanism is proven end to end with real money. This gates launch.
