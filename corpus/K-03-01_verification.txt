1. Doctrine
Before any field is specified, four positions must be settled, because every design choice below follows from them.
1.1 Verification does not allocate work
This is the most important sentence in the document. A professional&apos;s tier records what has been evidenced about their capability. It does not entitle them to work, and it does not exclude them from it. Work allocation is a separate decision driven by fit to the specific scope, availability, client requirement and price. [RULE — Constitution R2]
This must be stated plainly to every registrant, at the start and at the end of the process, because the alternative is corrosive. If people believe the tier is the queue for work, three things follow: they misrepresent themselves on the form, they experience every tier decision as a rejection, and any allocation that does not follow tier order looks like favouritism. Stating the separation honestly is what makes honest registration rational.
1.2 The ladder is built for ascent
Nobody is rejected for being early in their career. A person who cannot yet evidence capability is placed at the tier their evidence supports and told exactly — specifically, not generically — what would move them up. The system&apos;s purpose is to move people up it, not to sort them at the door. [Constitution B4]
1.3 Registration is the first act of the relationship
A registration form is normally an administrative cost imposed on the applicant. Ours is the first thing a professional experiences of Kayan, and it will shape whether they trust us with their identity documents and their reputation. It must therefore do four jobs at once: collect what we need, explain what we are, teach how the system works, and earn the honesty we are asking for.
Every section therefore opens with a plain statement of what it is for and why we are asking. A person who understands why a question is asked answers it accurately. [JUDGEMENT]
1.4 Ask once, ask late, ask only what is used
No field exists unless a named process consumes it. Fields that are only needed later are asked later — bank details are not collected at registration from someone who may never be engaged. This shortens the form, reduces abandonment, and reduces the personal data we are obliged to protect.

Kayan verifies professionals and delivers work for clients under a written scope with the client&apos;s money held in escrow. We contract with the client and stand behind delivery.
What verification means
We confirm you are who you say you are, and we assess what you can do, in specific areas, to a stated level. We record it, and we stand behind it to clients.

3. The Kayan competency framework
Two axes. Craft competence is specific to a micro-specialisation and is assessed per specialisation. Core competence is common to everyone who works through Kayan and describes how the work is done rather than what the work is.
Six core competencies. They are deliberately few, observable, and phrased as behaviour rather than personality — because personality cannot be assessed fairly and behaviour can.

4.2 Maintenance, lapse, downgrade and appeal
Mechanism
Rule
Notification
At 90, 30 and 7 days before expiry, with the specific action required. Automated — event E18.
Lapse
On expiry without action, the tier is reduced and the specialisation marked dormant. Removal from available capacity is automatic — event E19. Lapse is not a judgement and carries no stigma; reinstatement is a light process.
Downgrade
Applied for cause: misrepresentation, sustained non-delivery, conduct breach, or a failed re-assessment. Always with a written reason recorded on the record.
Immediate withdrawal
For fraud, identity misrepresentation, or a safeguarding breach. Decided by two approvers, one at executive level.
Appeal
Any adverse decision may be appealed once, in writing, within 21 days. Heard by someone who was not part of the original decision. Outcome and reasoning recorded on the record.
Reinstatement
A withdrawn record may apply again only after a stated period and only with new evidence. The original decision remains on the record permanently.
An appeal route is not a courtesy. A credential that cannot be challenged is not a standard, it is an opinion — and it will not withstand the scrutiny of the institutional clients we intend to sell to. [JUDGEMENT]

5.1 Why scenarios rather than a personality questionnaire
A questionnaire asking whether someone is reliable measures whether they know that reliability is desirable. A scenario asking what they would do when a deliverable will be two days late and the client has not been told measures judgement. All situational items are set in conditions our professionals actually face — a power cut before a deadline, a client changing scope verbally, a pod member going silent, a deliverable that is finished but not reviewed. [JUDGEMENT]

6. The reference verification system
References are the highest-value and most-abused element of any verification system. Ours is fully automated in mechanism and deliberately strict in eligibility.

6.2 The automated flow
The person provides referee name, role, organisation, relationship, email and phone. The system checks the eligibility rules immediately and refuses an ineligible referee with the specific reason.
The system sends a bilingual request to the referee containing a single-use token link. The referee does not create an account and does not log in — friction here means no response.
The link opens a structured form, expiring after 14 days. Reminders are sent automatically at day 3, day 7 and day 12.
The referee completes the form and confirms a short declaration. The response is written to the record and scored.
If no response is received by expiry, the person is notified and invited to substitute a referee. Non-response is never held against the person — it is a fact about the referee.
A random sample of referees is telephoned by a verification officer each month, and every reference supporting a T3 or T4 decision is contacted directly.

6.4 Fraud controls
Referee email domain checked against the stated organisation; free-mail addresses flagged for a telephone check.
Device and network signals compared between the registrant&apos;s session and the referee&apos;s; a match is flagged for human review, never auto-rejected.
Referees appearing across an unusual number of unrelated applications are flagged and investigated.
Reciprocal referencing between two registrants is blocked at the point of entry.
Response timing far outside normal ranges is flagged.
Content of a reference is never disclosed to the person it concerns. The person sees only whether a response was received. This is stated in advance to both parties, because a referee who believes the subject will read their response does not give a candid one. [RULE]

9. The client registration journey
A different design problem entirely. A professional will spend an hour registering because the payoff is theirs. A client will abandon a form in ninety seconds. Therefore: qualify fast, contract carefully, and place compliance at the contract rather than at the door. [JUDGEMENT]

10. The sponsor registration journey
Sponsors of Forge cohorts and of Kayan for Yemen programmes are a third counterparty with a third logic. They are not buying delivery; they are buying evidenced outcomes and the ability to report them onward to their own boards and funders.
Stage
What is asked
Note
1. Interest
Organisation, contact, thematic priority, geography, indicative scale, timing
Short. A sponsor is qualifying us as much as we are qualifying them.
2. Fit
Which programmes match their priorities, and what outcomes they are accountable for reporting
Their reporting obligation is the design input for our programme design
3. Due diligence — both ways
Our registration, governance, policies, financial controls and safeguarding provided; their requirements received
Institutional sponsors will assess us. Being ready for that assessment is itself a competitive advantage.
4. Agreement
Programme scope, funded outcomes, reporting calendar, payment schedule, branding and visibility terms
Obligations become calendar entries on the sponsor page
5. Delivery
Programme runs; participation is recorded against individual records
This is what makes absorption computable per sponsor
6. Reporting
Outcomes reported against the agreed calendar, including the Absorption Ratio attributable to that funding
The report is generated from records, never assembled by hand — rule DQ8
The competitive insight is that most implementing organisations report activity — people trained, sessions held. Kayan reports absorption — people who entered recorded, paid, contracted work — because the delivery system that produces the outcome is the same system that records it. Almost nobody else can do this, and it should be the centre of every sponsor conversation. [JUDGEMENT]
